About

Hey, I’m the solo dev behind Friends And Foes. I make survival games with a twist — NPCs that aren’t just bots.

Currently building a survival game with advanced NPCs that have real emotions. They get happy, angry, scared. They remember what you do. They choose whether you’re a friend or a foe.

Based in Rajkot, IN. Everything here is built solo, from code to art to sound.
